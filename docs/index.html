How Table for You picks 10 restaurants out of 7,581Table for You 如何从 7,581 家餐厅中挑出 10 家

Table for You recommends restaurants in Chicago. You say what you feel like eating and where you are, and it returns a short list with a reason for each pick. This page explains how it works for readers who have never built a recommender: where the data comes from, the simple methods it has to beat, the models we tried, how we tested them, and what we kept.

Table for You 是一个芝加哥餐厅推荐系统。你说出想吃什么、人在哪里，它返回一份简短的名单，并给出每家店的推荐理由。这一页写给从没做过推荐系统的读者，从头讲清楚：数据从哪里来、模型必须先打败哪些简单方法、我们试了哪些模型、怎样检验它们，以及最后留下了什么。

The big idea: narrow down, then sort carefully核心思路：先粗筛，再精排

A recommender guesses which few items, out of many, a person is most likely to enjoy. Running an expensive model on every restaurant for every request would be slow, so large systems work like a funnel. Hard rules first remove anything that breaks what you asked for. Cheap methods then pull a couple of hundred plausible candidates out of what is left. Finally, a more careful ranking model sorts the candidates, and you see the top 10.

推荐系统要做的，是在大量选项里猜出某个人最可能喜欢的那几个。每次请求都用一个昂贵的模型给所有餐厅打分太慢，所以大型系统都像漏斗一样工作：先用硬性规则去掉不符合你要求的；再用便宜的方法从剩下的餐厅里快速捞出一两百个候选（这一步叫“召回”）；最后用更精细的排序模型给候选排序，你看到的是前 10 名。

Video, shopping and food-delivery apps are built the same way. Table for You builds every stage on real data, so each one can be measured.

视频、电商和外卖应用都是这样搭建的。Table for You 用真实数据把每一级都搭了出来，所以每一级都可以被测量。

The data: 1.33 million Google Maps ratings数据：133 万条 Google 地图评分

The project uses Google Local Data (2021), a public research dataset released by the McAuley Lab at UC San Diego. It was collected from Google Maps and covers reviews and business details across the United States up to September 2021. We use only the Illinois files, and only restaurants in Chicago and Evanston. Credits and terms are in Data & copyright.

项目使用的是 Google Local Data (2021)，这是加州大学圣地亚哥分校（UCSD）McAuley 实验室公开发布的研究数据集，采集自 Google 地图，覆盖全美截至 2021 年 9 月的评论和商家信息。我们只用其中伊利诺伊州的文件，并且只保留芝加哥和 Evanston 的餐厅。数据署名和使用条款见数据来源与版权。

Each rating is a small record. Restaurants also come with details: name, categories such as “Pizza restaurant”, map coordinates, price level ($ to $$$$), opening hours, a short description and attribute groups such as Atmosphere and Service options.

每条评分都是一条很小的记录。每家餐厅还带有详细信息：名称、类别（如 “Pizza restaurant”）、地图坐标、价位（$ 到 $$$$）、营业时间、简短描述，以及“氛围”“服务方式”等属性分组。

Most ratings are five stars大多数评分是 5 星

57% of ratings are 5 stars and only about 7% are 1 or 2 stars, so a single star rating says little on its own. We count a rating of 4 or 5 as liked (82% of ratings). A restaurant someone never rated is unknown, not disliked: they may simply never have written a review.

57% 的评分是 5 星，1 星和 2 星加起来只有约 7%，所以单看一个星级说明不了太多。我们把 4 星和 5 星算作喜欢（占全部评分的 82%）。一个人没评过的餐厅是“未知”，不是“不喜欢”：他可能只是从没写过评价。

Ground rules for the data数据处理原则

  • Google account IDs are dropped when the data is loaded. People become anonymous numbers.
  • No review text and no photos are used. Only the star rating and its date.
  • Ratings stop at the end of 2019, so pandemic-era behavior (2020–2021) stays out of the test.
  • Google's own average rating and review count are not used. They include ratings written after the test period, which the model would not have known in 2019.
  • The “permanently closed” flag was recorded in 2021. It hides closed places at serving time but is never a model input, because it would tell the model which 2019 restaurants were about to close.
  • 加载数据时丢弃 Google 账号 ID，用户只保留匿名编号。
  • 不使用评论文字，也不使用照片，只用星级和日期。
  • 评分截止到 2019 年底，让疫情期间（2020–2021 年）的行为不进入测试。
  • 不使用 Google 给出的平均分和评论数。它们包含测试期之后写的评分，而模型在 2019 年是不可能知道这些的。
  • “永久歇业”标记记录于 2021 年。它只在上线时用来隐藏已关门的餐厅，从不作为模型输入，否则模型就会知道哪些 2019 年的餐厅即将关门。

A second, much smaller set of 66 Evanston restaurants and 1,436 ratings, where 92% of people have only one rating, tests the cold-start case: the system knows almost nothing about you.

另有一个小得多的数据集：Evanston 的 66 家餐厅、1,436 条评分，其中 92% 的用户只评过一次。它用来测试冷启动，也就是系统几乎不了解你的情况。

How we grade a recommender怎样给推荐系统打分

A fair test pretends we are back in mid-2019. Models learn only from ratings up to 30 June 2019. July to mid-September is used to choose between versions. The final exam is the last three months of 2019, and each model takes it once.

公平的检验方法是假装回到 2019 年年中：模型只能从 2019 年 6 月 30 日之前的评分中学习；7 月到 9 月中旬用来在不同版本之间做选择；最后的“期末考试”是 2019 年最后三个月，每个模型只考一次。

One test case is one person on one day一个测试样本 = 某个人的某一天

For each person and day in the test period, the model sees only what happened before that day and ranks all 7,581 restaurants. We then check where the restaurants that person rated 4 or 5 stars that day landed in the model's top 10. The test has 20,000 such cases from 10,016 people.

对测试期里的每个“人 + 日期”，模型只能看到那一天之前发生的事，然后给全部 7,581 家餐厅排序。接着看这个人当天打了 4 星或 5 星的餐厅，落在了模型前 10 名的哪个位置。测试集共有 20,000 个这样的样本，来自 10,016 个人。

Two scores: Recall@10 and NDCG@10两个分数：Recall@10 和 NDCG@10

Recall@10 asks how many of the liked restaurants made the top 10. NDCG@10 also cares about position: a hit at #1 counts fully and a hit lower down counts less. Both run from 0 to 1. Try it below.

Recall@10（召回率）问的是：喜欢的餐厅有几家进了前 10？NDCG@10 还看位置：排第 1 得满分，越往后得分越少。两个分数都在 0 到 1 之间。下面可以动手试试。

Why the real scores look tiny为什么真实分数看起来很小

The best model scores 0.0315 NDCG@10. That sounds poor until you see the task: pick 10 out of 7,581 for people who usually rate one or two places on a given day, and who visit many places they never review. Picking at random would reach a Recall@10 of about 0.0013 (10 ÷ 7,581, calculated rather than measured). The best model reaches 0.056, roughly 40 times that. What matters is the comparison with baselines on the same test.

最好的模型 NDCG@10 只有 0.0315，乍看很低，但要看任务有多难：从 7,581 家里挑 10 家，而一个人一天通常只评一两家店，还有很多去过却没评价的店。随机乱选的 Recall@10 约为 0.0013（10 ÷ 7,581，是算出来的而不是实测的）；最好的模型是 0.056，大约是随机的 40 倍。真正有意义的，是在同一套测试下和基线比较。

Error bars误差范围

Every test score comes with a 95% range. We rebuild the test set many times by drawing people at random with replacement (a bootstrap) and watch how much the score moves. If two models' ranges overlap, we do not call either one the winner on accuracy.

每个测试分数都附有一个 95% 区间。做法叫自助法（bootstrap）：从测试用户中有放回地随机抽样，重复很多次，观察分数会波动多少。如果两个模型的区间重叠，就不宣布谁的精度更高。

Baselines: the simple methods to beat基线：先要打败的简单方法

A baseline is the simplest reasonable answer. If a complex model cannot beat it, the complexity is not paying for itself. We ran three, all scored on the same test.

基线就是最简单、但说得过去的方法。如果复杂模型连它都打不过，复杂就没有意义。我们跑了三个，用的是同一套测试。

Highest average rating平均分最高

Baseline基线
How it works做法
Recommend the places with the best average stars, pulled toward the city-wide average so that a place with two 5-star ratings does not top the list (a Bayesian average).
推荐平均星级最高的餐厅，并把平均分往全市均值方向“拉”，免得只有两条 5 星评价的店排到最前（贝叶斯平均）。
Result结果
0.0008 NDCG@10, close to random. It surfaces small places with a handful of perfect reviews that most people never visit.
NDCG@10 0.0008，接近随机。它推出来的是只有几条满分评价的小店，大多数人根本不会去。

Most popular最热门

Baseline基线
How it works做法
Recommend the 10 restaurants with the most ratings in the training period, to everyone.
所有人都推荐训练期内评分条数最多的 10 家餐厅。
Result结果
0.0106 NDCG@10, 13 times better than average rating. This is the bar every personalized model has to clear. It shows the same 10 places to everyone.
NDCG@10 0.0106，是“平均分最高”的 13 倍。这是所有个性化模型都必须超过的分数。但它给所有人推荐的都是同样 10 家。

Similar descriptions (content-based)描述相似（基于内容）

Baseline基线
How it works做法
A pretrained language model, MiniLM, turns each restaurant's name, categories, description and tags into a list of 384 numbers (an embedding). Similar text gives similar numbers. The system recommends places whose numbers sit closest to the ones you liked.
用预训练语言模型 MiniLM，把每家餐厅的名称、类别、描述和标签转换成 384 个数字（称为嵌入向量）。文字越相似，数字越接近。系统推荐与你喜欢的店最接近的餐厅。
Result结果
0.0037 NDCG@10. Weak on its own in Chicago, where location and habit matter more than descriptions. But it covers far more of the catalog (79% of restaurants appear in someone's top 10), it works for a restaurant with no ratings yet, and on the small Evanston set it beat older TF-IDF word counting by 55%.
NDCG@10 0.0037。在芝加哥单独使用很弱，因为位置和习惯比描述更重要。但它覆盖的餐厅多得多（79% 的餐厅出现在某人的前 10 名里），还没有评分的新店也能推荐；在 Evanston 小数据集上，它比传统的 TF-IDF 词频方法高 55%。

The models we built我们搭建的模型

Each model below fills one stage of the funnel. For each one: what it does, an everyday comparison, and what we measured on the test period.

下面每个模型负责漏斗中的一级。每个都会讲：它做什么、一个生活中的类比，以及在测试期测到的结果。

Two-tower retrieval双塔召回模型

Candidates召回
What it does做什么
Two small neural networks. One turns you into 64 numbers from your taste (built from the restaurants you liked) and where you usually eat. The other turns each restaurant into 64 numbers from its text, categories and location. The match score is how well the two lists line up (a dot product). Restaurants can be scored ahead of time, so matching against all 7,581 takes about a millisecond. There is no per-person memory, so it also works for someone it has never seen, given a few picks.
两个小型神经网络。一个根据你的口味（由你喜欢过的餐厅算出）和你常去的区域，把“你”变成 64 个数字；另一个根据文字、类别和位置，把每家餐厅也变成 64 个数字。匹配分数就是两组数字有多“对齐”（点积）。餐厅那一侧可以提前算好，所以和全部 7,581 家做匹配只需约 1 毫秒。它不为每个人单独存参数，所以只要给出几家喜欢的店，从没见过的新用户也能用。
Everyday comparison类比
A matchmaker who writes a profile for each side in the same vocabulary, then compares profiles.
像一位红娘，用同一套词汇给双方各写一份档案，再比较两份档案有多合拍。
Result结果
0.0298 NDCG@10 on its own, about 2.8× most popular. The live two_tower option mixes its score 85/15 with the restaurant's average rating: 0.0294.
单独使用时 NDCG@10 为 0.0298，约为“最热门”的 2.8 倍。线上的 two_tower 选项把它的分数和餐厅平均分按 85/15 混合，得分 0.0294。

The training fix that mattered most: logQ correction最关键的训练技巧：logQ 修正

Training训练
The problem问题
To learn, the model sees a real visit and must pick that restaurant out from the restaurants of the other 1,023 visits in the same training batch, which act as wrong answers. Popular restaurants show up in batches far more often, so the model is constantly told they are wrong and learns to avoid them.
训练时，模型看到一次真实的光顾，要从同一批次里其他 1,023 条记录的餐厅中把这家店挑出来，其他餐厅都当作“错误答案”。热门餐厅在批次中出现得多得多，于是模型总被告知它们是错的，最后学会了躲开它们。
The fix修正
During training, subtract the log of each restaurant's popularity from its score, removing the head start it gets from appearing often.
训练时，从每家餐厅的分数中减去其热度的对数，抵消它因出现频繁而得到的“先天优势”。
Everyday comparison类比
A quiz where the most common answer is usually marked wrong teaches you never to give it. The fix tells the grader how common each answer is.
如果一场测验里最常见的答案总被判错，你就会学会永远不选它。修正的做法是告诉阅卷人每个答案本来有多常见。
Result结果
NDCG@10 rose from 0.0118 to 0.0298, about 2.5×, and matched the slower exact method that compares against all 7,581 restaurants (0.0316, overlapping ranges).
NDCG@10 从 0.0118 提高到 0.0298，约为 2.5 倍，并追平了与全部 7,581 家逐一比较的精确方法（0.0316，区间重叠），而后者更慢。

Semantic IDs语义 ID

Restaurant identity物品表示
What it does做什么
Giving every restaurant its own learned vector lets a model memorize it, but costs one row of parameters per restaurant and does nothing for new ones. A Semantic ID is a three-part code built by clustering restaurants on their content: the first part is a broad group, the second narrows it, the third narrows it again. Restaurants that share the start of a code share parameters. We used 3 levels of 64 codes (RQ-KMeans), giving 2,890 distinct codes for 7,581 restaurants. Chain branches with identical descriptions share a code.
给每家餐厅单独学一个向量，能让模型“记住”它，但每家店都要占一行参数，对新店也毫无帮助。语义 ID 是一个三段式编码：先按内容把餐厅聚类，第一段是大类，第二段再细分，第三段更细。编码开头相同的餐厅共享参数。我们用了 3 层、每层 64 个码（RQ-KMeans），7,581 家餐厅得到 2,890 个不同编码；描述完全相同的连锁分店共用一个编码。
Everyday comparison类比
A library call number: books with nearby numbers sit on nearby shelves and cover similar subjects.
像图书馆的索书号：编号相近的书放在相邻的书架上，内容也相近。
Result结果
Same accuracy as one vector per restaurant (0.0318 vs 0.0318 NDCG@10 in the two-tower) with 47% fewer embedding rows: 4,039 instead of 7,581.
与“每家店一个向量”精度相同（双塔中 NDCG@10 都是 0.0318），但嵌入表少了 47% 的行：4,039 行，而不是 7,581 行。

Sequential Transformer序列 Transformer

Offline experiment离线实验
What it does做什么
Reads your last 50 restaurants in order and predicts the next one, the way a language model predicts the next word. This is the idea behind recent “generative recommendation” systems in industry. Ours is small: 2 layers and 2 attention heads, in the style of SASRec.
按时间顺序读取你最近去过的 50 家餐厅，预测下一家，就像语言模型预测下一个词。这是近年业界“生成式推荐”系统背后的思路。我们的版本很小：2 层、2 个注意力头，采用 SASRec 的结构。
Everyday comparison类比
Guessing the next song from your recent playlist.
根据你最近的歌单猜下一首歌。
Result结果
0.0299 NDCG@10 with Semantic IDs. It matches the two-tower but does not beat it. With 1.3 million ratings the heavier model is not yet worth its cost, so it stays an offline experiment.
加上语义 ID 后 NDCG@10 为 0.0299，追平但没有超过双塔。在 133 万条评分的规模下，更重的模型还不值得它的成本，所以只作为离线实验保留。

Candidate sources + LightGBM ranker多路召回 + LightGBM 排序

Ranking排序Ships已上线
What it does做什么
Four sources nominate their best picks among the restaurants that pass your rules: the two-tower (100), content similarity (50), most popular (50) and nearest to you (50). That is about 220 after removing duplicates. LightGBM, a fast library for ensembles of decision trees, then scores each candidate from 14 clues:
two-tower score & rankcontent score & rankpopularity in trainingpopularity in its last 90 daysBayesian average ratingdistance from youshare of your liked places with this cuisineprice levelgap to your usual priceopen latehow many places you ratedyour average rating
四路召回各自在符合你条件的餐厅中提名：双塔（100 家）、内容相似（50 家）、最热门（50 家）、离你最近（50 家），去重后约 220 家。然后用 LightGBM（一个快速的决策树集成库）根据 14 个特征给每个候选打分：
双塔分数与排名内容分数与排名训练期热度最后 90 天热度贝叶斯平均分离你的距离你喜欢的店中同菜系的比例价位与你常去价位的差距是否营业到晚上你评过多少家店你的平均评分
Everyday comparison类比
Hiring: several recruiters each bring a shortlist, and one interviewer weighs all the evidence on every candidate.
像招聘：几位招聘官各自带来一份候选名单，再由一位面试官综合所有信息，逐个打分。
What we learned发现
  • The textbook ranking loss, LambdaRank, lost to simply ordering candidates by the two-tower score (0.0286 vs 0.0297 on held-out validation users). With one or two liked places among about 220 candidates, its pairwise signal is too thin. A plain pointwise loss, which predicts “liked or not” for each candidate, won at 0.0303 and is what ships.
  • The two-tower rank accounts for 53% of the trees' total gain, a measure of how much each clue is used. Distance is second at 9%. Removing distance costs only 3%, because location already enters through the two-tower and the nearby source.
  • The candidate step sets a ceiling: the four sources contain only about 32% of the restaurants people actually liked, and no ranker can recover the rest.
  • 教科书式的排序损失 LambdaRank，反而输给了“直接按双塔分数排序”（在留出的验证用户上 0.0286 对 0.0297）。约 220 个候选里只有一两个是用户喜欢的，成对比较的信号太少。简单的逐点损失（对每个候选预测“喜不喜欢”）以 0.0303 胜出，线上用的就是它。
  • 双塔排名占了所有决策树总增益的 53%（增益衡量每个特征被使用的程度）；距离排第二，占 9%。去掉距离只损失 3%，因为位置信息已经通过双塔和“附近”召回进入了模型。
  • 召回这一步设了天花板：四路候选只包含用户实际喜欢的餐厅中的约 32%，剩下的任何排序模型都救不回来。
Result结果
0.0315 NDCG@10, the highest of all, and the widest variety among the personalized models: 34% of restaurants appear in someone's top 10. About 7 ms per request.
NDCG@10 0.0315，所有方法中最高；在个性化模型里推荐的种类也最多：34% 的餐厅出现在某人的前 10 名里。每次请求约 7 毫秒。

Business rules业务规则

Rules规则Ships已上线
What it does做什么
Hard filters run before anything else: cuisine, maximum price level, budget, open after 8 pm, maximum distance, and permanently closed. Missing information never counts as a match, so a restaurant with an unknown price never passes a price filter. If nothing passes, the system says so and suggests relaxing a filter instead of quietly dropping one.
硬性过滤最先执行：菜系、最高价位、预算、晚上 8 点后是否营业、最远距离，以及是否永久歇业。缺失的信息永远不算符合，所以价位未知的餐厅不会通过价位过滤。如果没有任何餐厅符合，系统会明说并建议放宽某个条件，而不是悄悄去掉条件。

Also in the repository仓库里还有

ANN indexANN 索引
Approximate nearest-neighbor search looks only at the most promising 20% of restaurants and still finds 94.7% of the exact top 100 (0.32 ms instead of 0.78 ms). Exact search over 7,581 restaurants is already fast, so the index is ready for a bigger catalog but not switched on.
近似最近邻（ANN）搜索只查看最有希望的 20% 餐厅，仍能找到精确前 100 名中的 94.7%（0.32 毫秒，而精确搜索为 0.78 毫秒）。7,581 家餐厅的精确搜索已经很快，所以这个索引为更大规模准备好了，但目前没有启用。
LLM rerankLLM 重排
This optional mode sends the top 20 to Claude, Anthropic's language model, in two different orders and merges the two rankings, because language models tend to favor whatever is listed first. Any failure falls back to the normal order. It is off by default and has no offline score; its effect would be measured with real users.
一个可选分支：把前 20 名以两种不同顺序发给 Anthropic 的语言模型 Claude，再合并两次排序，因为语言模型往往偏爱排在前面的选项。任何失败都会退回原来的顺序。它默认关闭，没有离线分数，效果需要用真实用户来衡量。

Results on the test period测试期结果

Six approaches ranked the full catalog for the same 20,000 test cases. Bars show NDCG@10 and the thin lines show the 95% range. Hover over or tab to a row for details.

六种方法在同样的 20,000 个测试样本上对全部餐厅排序。柱子表示 NDCG@10，细线表示 95% 区间。把鼠标移到某一行（或用 Tab 键选中）可查看详情。

  • The ranker scores about 3× the most-popular baseline, and its range does not overlap with any baseline.
  • The three models are close. The ranker's lead over the two-tower blend (0.0315 vs 0.0294) is small and their ranges overlap, so accuracy alone does not settle it. The ranker also shows 1.8× the variety (34% vs 19% of the catalog) and uses distance and price directly.
  • Popularity has to mean how often people go. Ranking by average stars is almost as bad as picking at random.
  • 排序模型约为“最热门”基线的 3 倍，且区间与所有基线都不重叠。
  • 三个模型很接近。排序模型相对“双塔 + 平均分混合”的领先（0.0315 对 0.0294）不大，两者区间有重叠，所以单看精度定不了胜负。不过排序模型推荐的种类是后者的 1.8 倍（34% 对 19%），而且直接用上了距离和价位。
  • “热门”必须指去的人多。按平均星级推荐，几乎和随机乱选一样差。

What ships, and why最终选择及原因

The live service offers several options, called arms, so they can be compared on real traffic. The default is the full funnel. Every choice below was made on the validation period and confirmed once on the test period. When two options were within noise on accuracy, other things decided: cost, variety, and whether the model can serve someone new.

线上服务提供几个可选的分支（arm），以便在真实流量上对比。默认使用完整的漏斗。下面每个选择都是在验证期做出、在测试期确认一次的。两个方案精度差距在误差范围内时，由其他因素决定：成本、多样性，以及能不能服务新用户。

After launch: learning from clicks上线之后：从点击中学习

Ratings only show where people went and chose to write a review. They never show what someone was offered and skipped. So the service logs every recommendation it shows, called an impression, with its position and model version, and accepts a click or favorite only if it matches a logged impression. Click-through rates per arm then work as an A/B test between options.

评分只能告诉我们人们去过、并且写了评价的店，看不到“推荐给他了但他没点”的情况。所以服务会记录每一次展示（称为曝光），包括位置和模型版本；只有能对上已记录曝光的点击或收藏才会被接受。各分支的点击率就构成了不同方案之间的 A/B 测试。

People click the top slot more just because it is on top (position bias). A ranker trained naively on clicks would learn to repeat whatever the old ranker put first. The repository includes a multi-task model (MMoE) with a separate position tower that absorbs this effect during training and is removed at serving time. On simulated logs it recovers the position effect we planted. It will be trained for real once there is real traffic.

人们更爱点第一位，仅仅因为它排在最上面（位置偏差）。直接用点击训练的排序模型，会学会重复旧模型的排序。仓库里有一个多任务模型（MMoE），用一个单独的“位置塔”在训练时吸收这种偏差，上线时去掉。在模拟日志上，它能还原我们预设的位置效应。等有了真实流量，才会真正训练它。

Limits局限

  • A review is only a stand-in for a visit. Places people went to without reviewing look the same as places they never went.
  • Restaurant details are a 2021 snapshot (categories, price, hours) applied to behavior from 2016 to 2019.
  • New users are under-represented in Chicago, because only people with 5 or more ratings are kept. The Evanston set covers that case, but it is small and its ranges are wide.
  • No offline score measures clicks. The online numbers in the README screenshots come from scripted demo traffic, not real users.
  • 评价只是光顾的替代信号。去过但没评价的店，看起来和没去过的店一样。
  • 餐厅信息是 2021 年的快照（类别、价位、营业时间），却用在 2016–2019 年的行为上。
  • 芝加哥数据里新用户偏少，因为只保留了评过 5 家以上的用户。Evanston 数据集覆盖了这种情况，但样本小，区间很宽。
  • 离线分数无法衡量点击。README 截图里的线上指标来自脚本生成的演示流量，不是真实用户。

Glossary术语表

Recommender system
Software that ranks items for a person, using what it knows about the person and the items.
Catalog
Every item the system can recommend. Here, 7,581 restaurants.
Candidate generation
The fast first step that picks a few hundred plausible items. Also called retrieval.
Ranking
The slower, more careful step that puts the candidates in order.
Embedding
A list of numbers that represents a person or an item, so that similar things get similar numbers.
Baseline
A simple method used as a yardstick for the complex ones.
Cold start
Recommending to a person, or recommending an item, with little or no history.
Data leakage
Letting a model see information it would not have when making a real prediction, such as future ratings.
Validation and test sets
Data held back from training. Validation is for choosing between models; test is for the final grade, used once.
Recall@10
The share of liked items that appear in the top 10.
NDCG@10
Like recall, but hits near the top count more.
Coverage
The share of the catalog that appears in at least one person's top 10.
Impression
One item shown to one person in one position.
A/B test
Splitting real traffic between options and comparing what people do.
推荐系统
根据对人和物品的了解，为某个人给物品排序的软件。
物品库（catalog）
系统能推荐的全部物品。这里是 7,581 家餐厅。
召回（candidate generation）
快速挑出几百个可能合适的物品的第一步，也叫检索。
排序（ranking）
更慢、更精细地给候选排定先后的一步。
嵌入向量（embedding）
用一串数字表示一个人或一个物品，相似的东西对应相近的数字。
基线（baseline）
用来衡量复杂方法的简单参照方法。
冷启动（cold start）
给几乎没有历史记录的人做推荐，或推荐几乎没有历史记录的物品。
数据泄漏（data leakage）
让模型看到真实预测时本不该有的信息，比如未来的评分。
验证集与测试集
训练时留出的数据。验证集用来在模型之间做选择；测试集用来打最后的分数，只用一次。
Recall@10
喜欢的物品中，出现在前 10 名的比例。
NDCG@10
类似召回率，但排得越靠前的命中，得分越高。
覆盖率（coverage）
至少出现在一个人前 10 名中的物品，占全部物品的比例。
曝光（impression）
某个物品在某个位置被展示给某个人一次。
A/B 测试
把真实流量分给不同方案，比较人们的实际行为。
