PROTOCOL

Global chronological split

Equal dates stay together. Training history is frozen at the split boundary. Validation selects parameters; test data is reserved for the final report.

COMPARABILITY

Same candidates, same targets

Popularity, content and Two Tower share one evaluation protocol. Target review text, demographics and demo events are excluded from model inputs.

SMALL-DATA REALITY

Warm and cold users, separately

Most reviewers have only one rating; warm-user samples are small. Query counts, user counts, Recall@5, NDCG@5 and coverage matter more than an assumed Two Tower win.

02 / RANK

Explainable ranking

Combine retrieval signals with training-only Bayesian ratings using a fixed, transparent rule. Show retrieval and final ranking scores separately.

03 / CONSTRAIN

Apply constraints

Filter by cuisine, cost and historical late-hours flags. Keep review-only restaurant identities and clearly label missing metadata.

04 / OBSERVE

Observe feedback

Bind impressions to request, position and model version. Clicks and favorites require a matching impression. Demo logs stay out of real evaluation.

COLD START

PRODUCTION NEXT

Complete workflow, limited scale

Production needs data rights, fresh metadata, real exposure feedback, online experiments and monitoring. Add ANN, feature platforms or learned ranking only when scale warrants them.
